Fallen Star is the first game I've coded using Godot. You can play the game online at https://llg137.itch.io/fallen-star!

**Description**  
*You're a small, shattered piece of a large star, falling and barely thriving on the small planet XEDc. Unfortunately for you, the solar beams have sucked too much energy from you-- and in return, brought more enemies. In order to save yourself, you must collect five "twinkle stars" from the land--- it's an easy feat... just make sure they never catch you.*
